An HVAC alarm is only the start of the work.

I built Uptime Desk to carry evidence from recorded sensor data through diagnosis, manager approval, and technician dispatch. The system checks every manual quotation before it can enter a work order. It keeps deterministic rules as the primary detector, records disagreement with the model, and blocks a case when no sourced lockout step exists.

The results include the losses as well as the wins. Rules beat the model on the lab economizer and the field RTU. The AI improved verified diagnosis and citation support against its keyword baseline. OR-Tools reduced lateness against nearest qualified in a dispatch scenario, while earliest available tied it on lateness. These are measured lab, field, and scenario results. They are not field savings.
