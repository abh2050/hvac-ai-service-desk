## Higgsfield generation plan

The approved plan caps Higgsfield usage at 5 credits. It generates four 16:9 Soul 2.0 stills at 1080p. The site creates slow camera motion with CSS and uses the real owner recordings for the walkthrough.

1. Opening persona and mechanical-room style frame.
2. Scene still at 5:40 a.m.
3. Before still with the trend, manual, and dispatch sheet.
4. Resolved closing still with the verified work-order screen.

The style frame will use a dark navy base, warm concrete neutral, and safety orange accent. The lighting will stay cool and pre-dawn with practical amber fixtures. The lens will feel like a restrained 35 mm documentary frame. The image texture will use clean editorial illustration with realistic industrial detail and no logos.

## Credit checkpoint

Higgsfield reported a balance of 10 credits on the Free plan. A no-generation preflight returned 0.12 credits per Soul 2.0 image, displayed as 1 credit after rounding. The approved four-image plan therefore costs 0.48 credits before display rounding and up to 4 credits if each image bills at the displayed rounded value. The owner capped the work at 5 credits.
